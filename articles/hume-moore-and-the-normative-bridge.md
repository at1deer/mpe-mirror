## Put the Bridge in the Middle of the Room.

[The current harm thesis](https://philpapers.org/rec/LAWHAC-3?ref=modalpathethics.com) is:

> **An actual transition harms an extant locus insofar as it nontrivially degrades a structurally significant continuation or enabling structure genuinely reachable from that locus.**

That sentence is carrying several jobs, and the first defense against both philosophers is to stop pretending they are one job.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Shirrako---Dark-Souls-Remastered---Ornstein---Smough-Boss-Fight--1080p-60fps--PS4-PRO--Nhb05wE2C5E---854x480---0m35s-.png)

A **locus** is an extant bearer capable of structured continuation.

A **reachable continuation** is a continuation genuinely available from that bearer under the actual field conditions.

Structural significance is constrained by features including enabling centrality, irreversibility, breadth, depth, resistance, distribution, and destructive potential. These are diagnostic dimensions, not terms in a master score or an exhaustive canonical vector. They expose different features of the damaged relation and support partial comparison only where the field actually supports comparison.

-   **_Then an actual transition happens._**

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Shirrako---Dark-Souls-Remastered---Ornstein---Smough-Boss-Fight--1080p-60fps--PS4-PRO--Nhb05wE2C5E---854x480---2m31s--1--1.png)

-   The body loses function.
-   The ecosystem loses reproductive structure.
-   The institution loses a correction path.
-   The person loses a viable continuation.
-   The archive is destroyed.
-   The relationship becomes coercively unavailable.
-   The planet-forming disk loses the conditions under which its organized continuation could proceed.

That gives us the structural judgment.

-   **Something happened to the bearer.**

Then comes the normative judgment.

> **Qualifying degradation of the structurally significant reachable continuation of an extant locus has pro tanto normative significance.**

Call this the **Continuance Principle**.

**_Pro tanto_** matters.

A defensive firebreak can damage vegetation while preventing a wider catastrophe. An evacuation can restrict movement while protecting the people being moved. One transition can contain a genuine harm-token and still be justified, required, or Better than every reachable alternative. Modal Path Ethics already separates the harm from the complete transition profile.

So here is the bridge.

## Ornstein Attacks.

Near the end of the first section of Book III of the _Treatise of Human Nature,_ Hume notices philosophers describing the world for a while and then, suddenly, producing propositions joined by **ought** and **ought not**. Something new has appeared. Hume's demand is that the new relation be identified and explained rather than allowed to slip unnoticed out of a chain of factual propositions.

This is a good demand.

Keep it.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Shirrako---Dark-Souls-Remastered---Ornstein---Smough-Boss-Fight--1080p-60fps--PS4-PRO--Nhb05wE2C5E---854x480---1m24s-.png)

If I tell you:

-   A forest lost half its mature canopy.
-   A person's mobility was permanently reduced.
-   A political institution lost its independent appeal route.
-   A species lost a major reproductive population.
-   A world lost a viable generative structure.

I have told you things about reality.

If I then conclude:

> **Therefore you ought not to have done that,**

something has happened.

That word **therefore** needs an account.

Modal Path Ethics does not hide from this.

The physical description does not contain an imperative punctuation mark waiting to be discovered under sufficient magnification.

This was already made explicit in the recent **Continuance Principle** work. The stripping of a planet-forming disk supplies a physical description. It does not deductively establish the ethical extension. The burden on the framework is explanatory: whether contraction identifies the common structure of paradigm harms, preserves the distinctions those cases require, and continues to work where subject-centered concepts stop fitting.

-   The bridge is a philosophical commitment.
    -   Hume has forced us to identify it.
        -   He has not yet destroyed it.

## The Tree Did Nothing Wrong.

Hume has a much funnier weapon waiting nearby.

> _Consider a tree._

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/image-264-1.png)

-   An oak drops a seed.
    -   The seed becomes a sapling.
        -   The sapling grows.
            -   Eventually it overtops and destroys the parent tree.

Hume asks what objective relation is missing here that would be present if a child killed a parent. **Cause** is present. **Destruction** is present. **Dependence** is present. The relevant relations can be described without producing the moral quality of **parricide**.

This is supposed to put pressure on the idea that vice can simply be found among objective relations.

Modal Path Ethics has an awkward response.

> **Yes.**

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Shirrako---Dark-Souls-Remastered---Ornstein---Smough-Boss-Fight--1080p-60fps--PS4-PRO--Nhb05wE2C5E---854x480---1m17s--1.png)

The sapling did **nothing** wrong.

We can clear that shit immediately.

No charges will be filed. No tribunal convened. The sapling will not be asked to reflect on its choices.

This is where Hume's example reveals a distinction Modal Path Ethics has spent a long time building.

> **Damage is not culpability.**

The question **what happened to the bearer?** precedes the question **who should be blamed for it?**

Hume is absolutely right that causal relations alone do not contain a little packet of guilt.

Modal Path Ethics does not need them to.

-   The oak is not wicked.
-   The parent tree can still be destroyed.

The framework's first claim concerns the second sentence.

Hume's argument is aimed at theories trying to find **vice** inside objective relations.

Modal Path Ethics has already moved vice downstream.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Minoas------------------Dark-Souls-1---Ornstein-and-Smough-NG-7--No-Damage--Silver-Knight--I1oTAZBUrOs---854x480---1m10s--1.png)

Blame, intention, responsibility, punishment, excuse, and desert belong to later analysis of agents situated inside the causal history.

First:

-   **What happened?**

Then:

-   **Who could see it?**
-   **Who could change it?**
-   **Who caused it?**
-   **What did they know?**
    -   **What should happen now?**

The tree did nothing wrong.

That does not resurrect the tree.

## Keep the Question Open.

The question remains intelligible.

A person can understand every word of the harm thesis and reject the Continuance Principle.

They can say:

-   I agree that this person's significant future was destroyed.
-   I agree that nothing comparable was preserved.
-   I agree that the loss was severe, irreversible, broad, asymmetrical, and concentrated on them.
    -   I still do not think that counts against the act.

Fine. Now we know exactly where we disagree.

That is progress.

Modal Path Ethics does not need to claim that **bad** is a dictionary synonym for **contraction**.

It makes a stronger structural proposal.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Boss-Fight-Database---Dark-Souls-Ornstein-and-Smough-Boss-Fight--QHDPr07hyzs---854x480---4m47s-.png)

Paradigm cases ordinarily called harms share an underlying relation: a bearer loses or is burdened in its capacity for structurally significant continuation.

The theory then assigns that relation normative significance through the Continuance Principle.

Moore can refuse the principle. If he wants.

He cannot defeat the structural account simply by observing that the normative question remains conceptually askable.

**Conceptual openness** and **metaphysical separation** are different claims.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Boss-Fight-Database---Dark-Souls-Ornstein-and-Smough-Boss-Fight--QHDPr07hyzs---854x480---5m23s-.png)

Someone could understand the word **water** without knowing its chemical constitution.

Someone could understand inheritance without knowing DNA.

Someone could recognize disease before possessing germ theory.

Discovering an underlying constitution need not make every ordinary question analytically closed in advance.

Modal Path Ethics is therefore not trying to prove:

> **harm means contraction**

as a linguistic identity.

It proposes:

> **qualifying damage to bearer-relative continuability constitutes the structural phenomenon our harm judgments have been tracking.**

Then:

> **the Continuance Principle gives that damage pro tanto normative force.**

## Moore Gets Hit by the Firebreak.

There is another reason the **Open Question** never disappears.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/20/43/2043f11a-6ae3-404c-bb28-01fce8d9ac88/content/images/2026/09/Boss-Fight-Database---Dark-Souls-Ornstein-and-Smough-Boss-Fight--QHDPr07hyzs---854x480---1m46s-.png)

> Suppose a firebreak destroys a strip of habitat.

-   **Was there harm?**
    -   Possibly yes.
-   **Should the firebreak have been cut?**
    -   That remains open.

> Suppose a surgeon amputates a limb to prevent death.

-   **Was a bodily continuation destroyed?**
    -   Obviously.
-   **Was the operation therefore wrong?**
    -   No such conclusion follows automatically.

> Suppose someone is evacuated from a home under emergency authority.

-   **Did the intervention close presently reachable choices?**
    -   Yes.
-   **Was it justified?**
    -   Ask what the alternatives were.

The framework does not need every identification of harm to conclude deliberation. It needs identification of harm to **begin honest deliberation**.

That is why harm-tokens survive justified action. The damaged vegetation does not become undamaged because the firebreak was wise. The lost limb does not reappear because the surgery saved a life. The displaced person did not stay home because evacuation turned out to be necessary.

A theory that can only justify an action by denying the injuries contained inside it has purchased moral clarity by falsifying the field.

Modal Path Ethics refuses the purchase.

## Two Souls.

Hume and Moore have now done something useful. They have forced the architecture into the open.

-   The field contains extant loci with structured continuations.
-   **Reachability** identifies which continuations genuinely belong to the present field.
-   **Weighting** constrains judgments of structural significance through features including enabling centrality, irreversibility, breadth, depth, resistance, distribution, and destructive potential; it does not produce a universal scalar.
-   The harm thesis identifies qualifying degradation to that continuability.
-   The **Continuance Principle** gives the harm-token pro tanto normative significance.
-   **Care** describes an agent's cultivated availability to that significance.
-   Deliberation examines the complete transition profile: harms, openings, repairs, burdens, constraints, uncertainty, alternatives, and remainder.
-   **Better** governs choice where clean **Good** is no longer reachable.

There is no secret slide from chemistry to command.

No dictionary trick in which **harm** is defined so conveniently that philosophy has already ended.

-   There is a structural claim.
-   There is a normative primitive.
-   There is an account of moral perception.
-   There is an account of damaged choice.
    -   And all of them can fail separately.

That is exactly what we should want from the theory.

-   Hume was right to watch the bridge.
-   Moore was right to inspect what crossed it.
    -   Neither philosopher establishes that ethics becomes more rigorous when **the bridge is demolished and everyone remains forever on the descriptive shore**.

Modal Path Ethics crosses. Openly.

It does so in public. Fucking stop me.
