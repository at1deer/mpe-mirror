# Applied Case: Physics Finds the Empty Altar

Last month, [psychology finally found the empty altar](https://modalpathethics.com/applied-case-psychology-finds-the-empty-altar/).

Psychology looked at the old sacred structure, correctly noticed that belief, ritual, meaning, grief, terror, hope, and God-talk can act inside the human psyche, and then began quietly moving the whole metaphysical problem into the patient.

Modal Path Ethics objected. The psyche participates in the contact. It does not own whatever the contact reaches.

The ruling was simple:

-   The sacred may enter the clinic.
-   The clinic may not become the cosmos.

**Physics** has now arrived at the altar from the other side. 

Steve Fuller, professor of social epistemology at the University of Warwick, has published an essay with the Institute of Art and Ideas under the **extremely calm** title:

> “_We don’t have different perspectives, we inhabit different realities._”

His argument begins from a serious problem.

Ordinary **realism** often imagines _reality_ as something already completed over there while the knower stands over here trying to copy it down correctly. The cleaner the copy, the better the knowledge. The observer’s participation looks very suspicious. If our cognitive equipment helps constitute what becomes available to us, realism begins worrying that somebody contaminated the pure _Reality_.

Fuller thinks developments in neuroscience, quantum mechanics, and generative artificial intelligence are making that picture harder to sustain. Brains are differently tuned. Quantum mechanics destabilizes the old observer-independent picture. Different conceptual schemes can reorganize what a person understands the world to contain. 

Fuller’s conclusion is much larger:

-   We do not simply encounter one reality differently.
    -   **We inhabit different realities.** 

Modal Path Ethics would like to inspect the promotion. Because physics has indeed found something missing from the altar.

It is the Controller.

* * *

## Contact.

Start by granting the strongest part.

> The fantasy of transparent observation is in **deep** trouble.

Human cognition does not stand outside reality collecting finished objects through a clean window. Bodies select. Nervous systems select. Languages select. Instruments select. Experimental designs select. Categories select. Historical training selects. Institutions decide what receives a sensor, what becomes data, what reaches a journal, and what somebody knows how to ask about in the first place.

The observer is already in the experiment.

This does not require quantum mechanics. Kant got there early enough to make everybody else’s version irritatingly late.

In _Kant and the Corrigible Field_, Modal Path Ethics accepted the critical move. Kant does not allow reason to leap from having a concept to possessing the corresponding structure of reality. Space, time, categories, synthesis, judgment: human cognition arrives with equipment. The world as known is encountered under conditions of contact.

The article’s formulation was:

-   Every known field is a field encountered through conditions of contact.

And then immediately after:

-   The known field should never be confused with a view from nowhere.

That was the **Kantian chastening**.

Then comes the sentence doing almost all the work over here:

> **That does not make the field unreal.** 

There is the hinge.

-   Fuller correctly attacks the view from nowhere.
-   The difficulty begins when the absence of _nowhere_ starts multiplying realities **everywhere**.

* * *

## Kant Was Already Standing Here, This Guy is Annoying.

There are two very tempting exits from conditioned contact.

The first says:

> **My instrument gives me reality as it is.**

-   The mind becomes a transparent window. 
    -   The theory follows the joints. 
    -   The model describes the world. 
        -   The instrument disappears from its own account.

[Kant](https://modalpathethics.com/kant-and-the-corrigible-field/) attacks this.

Modal Path Ethics assails it too.

The second exit arrives after the attack:

-   If every contact is conditioned, there is no shared reality beneath the conditions.
    -   Now the instrument returns with even greater authority.
    -   Instead of transparently reporting reality, it begins furnishing one.

The observer has lost the God's-eye view and received a fullblown universe in compensation.

That is **way** too generous.

Kantian humility establishes a limit on possession. It does not automatically establish **ontological multiplication**. Kant's own legacy is considerably stranger and more contested than a simple transition from one reality to many: the distinction between appearances and things in themselves has supported radically different readings for more than two centuries. 

Modal Path Ethics needs even less than Kant gives us. It doesn't ask for very much. It does not need final access to the thing-in-itself.

It just needs contact capable of correction.

A bridge still collapses under one description and another. A tumor continues growing under competing conceptual schemes. A particle detector produces the event its apparatus makes measurable, under physical constraints the researcher does not get to negotiate away afterward. A person can be misdescribed by five institutions and remain the person all five descriptions failed to carry.

The instrument participates. The field answers.

That is enough.

## Barontini Rips the Fucking Clock From the Wall.

Then Giovanni Barontini built the perfect little physical object for this argument.

In June, Barontini published _Testing the problem of time with cold atoms_ in Physical Review Research.

He took a Bose-Einstein condensate and partitioned it using an optical barrier into an observed bright sector and an unobserved dark sector. The system was sufficiently isolated for the experiment to ask a very old and very weird question in very controlled form:

-   Can one part of a system acquire a usable ordering of events without relying on a clock outside the system?
    -   **Yes**.

Barontini constructed an internal time from coarse-grained entropy exchange between the sectors. That internal variable successfully ordered repeated expansion and recollapse of the observed sector and supported an effective Schrödinger description reproducing the measured evolution. The paper presents the system as a controlled experimental setting for testing relational constructions of time. 

Modal Path Ethics [previously reacted to this](https://modalpathethics.com/transition-action-the-clock-becomes-an-entropy-leak/) with the appropriate level of restraint. Meaning I called Barontini **Creator of the Bright Sector**, **Lord of the Barrier Height**, **Grand Watcher of the Oscillating Cloud**, **Source of the Great Division**.

There was science underneath all this. The clock had moved.

Ordinary intuition imagines **time** being supplied from outside: a universal parameter against which everything else happens. Barontini’s experiment demonstrates that an experimentally useful ordering variable can instead emerge through a relation internal to an effectively isolated system.

The earlier article gave this result its official Modal Path Ethical form:

-   The clock is not a sovereign object.
-   The clock is another relation under accounting conditions. 

This is also exactly the wrong experiment from which to conclude that reality has become subjective.

Because look at what happened.

-   The boundary mattered.
-   The choice of observed sector mattered.
-   The coarse-graining mattered.
-   The relation between sectors mattered.
-   The external researcher still mattered.
    -   And the researcher did not get to choose what entropy exchange occurred after setting the apparatus.

The description was **conditioned** without becoming **arbitrary**.

The internal time was relational without becoming imaginary.

The bright sector acquired an ordering through its relation to something the selected description deliberately left dark. That dark sector did not disappear because the instrument stopped looking at it.

In fact, the visible ordering depended upon the relation to what had been excluded from direct observation.

That is the best model for the present problem.

A selecting cut can constitute the reading without constituting the whole field.

## The Dark Sector Is Still There, Still Dark, Still Waiting.

This was the part of the Barontini article that mattered most.

The bright sector is only readable because a boundary has been drawn. The boundary produces a useful reduced description.

-   The reduction leaves something out.
-   What it leaves out continues acting.

The dark sector is therefore neither an embarrassment to the measurement or its colleagues nor is it a second private universe belonging to somebody who happened to stand on the other side of the laboratory. It is part of the relation making the bright sector intelligible.

Modal Path Ethics called the risk here **bright-sector narcissism**:

> The observed sector may be where the data lives, but the observed sector is not the whole field.

And then:

> The boundary creates the reading. 

And there is our route out of the old realism dispute.

-   We do not need the observer outside the universe.
-   We do not need a cosmic clock.
-   We do not need one final vocabulary capable of describing everything from nowhere.
-   We also do not need to give every observer a personal replacement cosmos.

**Different observers can carry different selecting cuts through a field larger than any one of them.**

Their descriptions may genuinely differ.

Their available variables may differ.

Their histories of contact may differ.

Their instruments may make different relations legible.

Sometimes one instrument can reveal something another cannot even articulate.

**None of this** requires reality to issue separate deeds.

## Physics Did Not Hand Out Tiny Thrones.

Fuller moves very quickly through quantum mechanics.

His essay says quantum mechanics presents a reality in which we inhabit many worlds at once.

That is already an interpretation wearing the theory’s nametag.

The Many-Worlds Interpretation is a serious interpretation of quantum mechanics. It is not simply what _quantum mechanics says_ before philosophers and physicists begin arguing about what its formalism means. Other interpretations make very different ontological commitments. Even within Many-Worlds, “**world**” has technical complications that do not straightforwardly map onto individual human beings inhabiting personalized realities. 

Relational quantum mechanics gets much closer to the philosophical pressure Fuller wants.

There, physical variables can take values relative to interactions between systems. Different observers can give different accounts of the same events. The observer does not need to be a conscious human; any relevant physical system can occupy the relational role.

That is a profound departure from the picture of properties sitting fully formed inside isolated objects waiting for somebody to inspect them.

This still does not give every observer a private universe.

Relational quantum mechanics explicitly speaks of **different accounts of the same set of events**. Relativity of a variable can work more like velocity: velocity is relative between systems without requiring every moving object to inhabit a separate reality. 

Even the strongest Wigner’s-friend arguments need care. No-go theorems have placed pressure on assumptions about observer-independent facts under specified conditions. Their interpretation and strength remain live subjects of dispute. Recent work has argued that some celebrated versions do not constrain physical reality as strongly as advertised. Leave the dispute open. That is exactly what a corrigible instrument does.

Physics is definitely allowed to be stranger than metaphysics.

## Galileo Did Not Move the Sun, I Looked It Up.

Fuller gives Galileo as another image of inhabiting different worlds.

-   Before the telescopic confrontation, one conceptual world.
    -   Afterward, another.

This is useful if **world** means something like a lived or conceptual world: the structured horizon through which facts acquire meaning, expectations become sensible, and observations fit together.

Modal Path Ethics has plenty of room for that. **Fictional Earth** is basically an industrial facility for studying it.

But ontological reality should not be smuggled across that semantic bridge. Please step into my office.

> Galileo’s conceptual transition did not move the Sun.

That is why the transition matters.

The heliocentric account could become superior because astronomical structure resisted the old map in increasingly expensive ways. Observation, prediction, mathematics, instrumentation, and later physics could carry more of the field.

-   A **model** died.
-   The **solar system** did not need to.

If geocentrism and heliocentrism constitute wholly separate **realities** in the strongest sense, the word _correction_ becomes difficult to preserve.

> **Corrected by _what_?**

The old description could simply belong to its reality. The new description belongs to another.

The telescope becomes a transportation device. This move is much weaker than science.

**Science** is only interesting because reality gets to ruin your theory.

## The Chastening of the Observer.

[_Transition Action_](https://modalpathethics.com/tag/transition-action/) has been following one recurring humiliation whenever it remembers to exist.

### _Control keeps leaving the throne._

-   _Shape turns out to carry computation._
-   _Load can move instead of waiting for one generator to dominate it._
-   _A living body becomes part of the actuator._
-   _A mechanical signal can produce response before becoming centralized data._
-   _The external clock becomes an internal relation._

Each case weakens the intuition that a capability must sit in a privileged controller standing outside the thing being controlled.

Barontini's case provided the explicit formulation:

> a chastening of the external controller. 

Fuller has now carried us to the next one to fall.

-   **The observer.**

The **old** observer sits in the control room.

-   Reality is outside.
    -   The observer looks.

The better the observer becomes, the less the conditions of looking are supposed to matter.

Eventually, the **perfect observer** acquires _Reality_ as one complete representation.

Physics has been extraordinarily rude to this fictional person.

Measurement enters the event. Frames enter it. Interactions enter it. Reference systems enter. Experimental boundaries enter.

Internal clocks can replace an imagined clock outside the whole system. Quantum foundations keep generating situations in which demanding there be one observer-independent account from nowhere creates conceptual trouble.

Excellent. Fire the controller.

This next step is important, though:

> **Do not replace him with eight billion smaller controllers.**

Fuller reaches the empty altar and starts handing out tiny thrones. Everybody gets a reality. Modal Path Ethics objects to the Winfrey distribution plan.

## Different Cuts Through One Enormous Field.

There is a stronger way to preserve what Fuller has found.

Human beings genuinely can inhabit different experiential worlds.

Neural architecture. History. Language. Culture. Trauma. Training. Bodies. Technology. Institutions.

Those differences are consequential enough without converting them into complete ontological separation.

Two people can receive different worlds from the same event because their available paths through it are different.

They can notice different relations. They can assign different salience. They can carry different histories into contact.

One may have access to an instrument the other lacks. One may inhabit an institution whose categories make part of the event visible while rendering another part nearly impossible to articulate.

These are real differences. They alter reachability and action. They alter what can be known next.

Modal Path Ethics does not need to flatten them into “different opinions.”

It can just call them what they are:

-   different selecting cuts through an extant field no single locus exhausts.

Sometimes the cuts overlap. Sometimes they conflict.

Sometimes one exposes what another buried. Sometimes one is catastrophically wrong.

Sometimes several remain useful because they carry different relations at different scales.

And sometimes [reality places a mine under Lake Peigneur](https://modalpathethics.com/tales-of-distortion-the-lake-peigneur-drilling-accident/) and resolves the argument with geological enthusiasm. 

The field remains exceptionally capable of feedback.

## The Ruling.

Steve Fuller has found a genuine crack in a familiar realism.

The knower cannot always be removed cleanly from knowledge.

The conditions of observation matter.

Different cognitive systems do not encounter the world through identical equipment.

Quantum physics supplies serious reasons to question an image of reality as a collection of fully determinate classical properties waiting beneath every perspective for the final observer to write them down.

Follow that result carefully.

-   The external controller disappears.
    -   The relation remains.
-   The sovereign observer disappears.
    -   The contact remains.
-   The universal perspective disappears.
    -   The field remains capable of answering.

Physics has found the empty altar. It did not find an empty universe.

> The absence of the controller does not imply the absence of the field.

There is no view from nowhere. There is also no deed issued to every somewhere.

The observer participates in reality. The observer conditions the reading.

The boundary makes something available. The dark sector still matters.

Another observer may arrive carrying another instrument and expose something the first one could not see.

No perspective has to exhaust extance for contact to remain real.

No instrument has to own reality for reality to correct the instrument.

> Reality does not need to possess a sovereign observer in order to constrain every observer.
